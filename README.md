# HobbyFlow

### Share Knowledge. Discover Stories. Connect Through Hobbies.

**HobbyFlow** is a modern, community-driven marketplace and digital platform designed for book lovers, collectors, comic enthusiasts, and board game fans.

Bringing together commerce, digital reading, and community engagement in one seamless ecosystem, HobbyFlow makes it easier to discover, buy, sell, lend, trade, and explore the things you love.

From individual books and curated book collections to comics and board games, the platform offers a comprehensive experience built around shared interests, meaningful connections, and a passion for discovery.

Beyond the marketplace, HobbyFlow features an integrated digital reader, a dedicated community hub, events, news, and an online store — creating a connected environment where hobbies become shared experiences.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 📚 Books & Book Collections

A comprehensive marketplace for discovering and exchanging literature.

- **Buy & Sell Books** — Publish listings, explore available titles, and connect directly with other readers.
- **Book Lending** — Borrow and lend physical books through a collateral-based lending system.
- **Book Collections** — Create, showcase, and sell curated collections, complete series, and multi-volume editions.
- **Book Trading** — Exchange books with other community members through a dedicated trading system.
- **Advanced Discovery** — Explore listings by genre, author, language, condition, price, and other parameters.
- **Personal Libraries & Favorites** — Save interesting titles and manage your favorite listings.

### 🦸 Comics Marketplace

A dedicated space for comic book enthusiasts and collectors.

- Discover comics across different genres and publishers.
- Buy, sell, and lend comic books.
- Explore detailed listings with descriptions, images, and publication information.
- Save favorite comics and share discoveries with others.

### 🎲 Board Games

Explore a marketplace built for tabletop gaming enthusiasts.

- Discover and browse board games.
- Publish listings to buy and sell games.
- Explore game descriptions, specifications, and images.
- Connect with other board game enthusiasts.

### 📖 HobbyFlow Reader

An integrated digital reading experience that allows users to enjoy electronic books directly within the platform.

- Support for multiple e-book formats, including PDF, EPUB, TXT, and FB2.
- Convenient reading interface with intuitive navigation.
- Full-screen reading experience.
- Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile devices.
- A clean, distraction-free environment focused on comfortable reading.

### 🌐 HobbyFlow Club

A dedicated community space that connects people through literature, collecting, and shared interests.

**News & Updates**
- Discover platform announcements, community news, and featured content.
- Stay informed about new features, updates, and upcoming activities.

**Events**
- Explore upcoming community events and activities.
- View detailed event information.
- Express interest in events and connect with other participants.

**Community**
- Participate in discussions and community conversations.
- Connect with readers, collectors, and hobby enthusiasts.
- Share experiences, recommendations, and ideas.

### 🛍 HobbyFlow Shop

An integrated online store offering a dedicated shopping experience within the HobbyFlow ecosystem.

- Browse products through a structured storefront.
- Explore product details and images.
- Add products to a shopping cart.
- Submit and manage orders through an intuitive interface.

### 👤 User Profiles & Marketplace Management

- Personalized user profiles and account management.
- Dedicated seller profiles with activity and reputation information.
- Listing creation, editing, and management.
- Order management and transaction status tracking.
- Notifications for orders, trades, and platform activities.
- Favorites, shopping cart, and user-specific content.

### 🎨 Modern User Experience

HobbyFlow is designed with a strong emphasis on usability, accessibility, and visual consistency.

- **Minimalist Interface** — Clean layouts, thoughtful typography, and intuitive interactions.
- **Responsive Design** — Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **Light & Dark Themes** — Adaptive appearance with system theme detection and manual switching.
- **Interactive Components** — Smooth animations, image galleries, sliders, and modal interfaces.
- **Real-Time Updates** — Dynamic content synchronization powered by Firebase.
- **Unified Ecosystem** — Consistent navigation across marketplace, reader, shop, and community features.

## 🛠 Technology Stack

HobbyFlow is built using a modern frontend architecture with cloud-based services and reusable UI components.

| Category | Technologies |
|---|---|
| Frontend | React.js, JavaScript |
| Build Tool | Vite |
| Styling | Tailwind CSS, SCSS |
| Routing | React Router DOM |
| State Management | React Hooks, Redux Toolkit |
| Backend & Database | Firebase, Firestore |
| Authentication | Firebase Authentication |
| Additional Services | Supabase, EmailJS |
| Notifications | React Toastify |
| UI & Interactions | Swiper.js, React Icons |

## 🎯 Project Vision

HobbyFlow aims to create a unified digital ecosystem where physical collections, digital content, and community interaction naturally come together.

The platform encourages users to give books a second life, discover new interests, exchange knowledge, build personal collections, and connect with people who share their passions.

By combining marketplace functionality, digital reading, and community-driven features, HobbyFlow goes beyond traditional buying and selling to deliver a more engaging and connected hobby experience.

**One Platform. Endless Stories. Shared Passions.**
